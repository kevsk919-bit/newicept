Institute of Computer Engineers and Pioneers of Technology

Mission

To equip students with a strong foundation in computer engineering and information technology through hands-on instruction, mentorship, and real-world project work, preparing them to enter the industry as capable, ethical, and adaptable professionals.

The program is committed to nurturing curiosity, discipline, and technical craftsmanship at every stage of a student's academic journey at PUP Lopez Campus.

Vision

To be recognized as a leading program in computer engineering education within the region, producing graduates who drive innovation, uphold professional integrity, and contribute meaningfully to the growth of the technology sector.

The program envisions a community of learners and educators working together to advance technical excellence for future generations.
