"Regalo't Saya, Para sa mga Bata Part 2" was a heartwarming outreach initiative held on December 16, 2023. Designed to bring festive joy to 120 underprivileged children in Lopez, Quezon, the program targeted 6- to 8-year-old youths from the barangays of Matinik, San Jose, Sta. Lucia, Hondagua, and Sta. Teresa. The event's core mission was to provide gifts, engaging entertainment, and a supportive platform to foster the children's emotional and social growth.

Organized by the PUP Lopez Diploma in Computer Engineering Technology faculty and the ICEPT student organization, the celebration took place at Matinik Elementary School. The young participants were treated to a lively afternoon packed with storytelling, fun games, and special intermission numbers. Beyond the engaging activities, the program successfully distributed essential items — including clothes, slippers, and toys — alongside meals and a raffle draw. Ultimately, the initiative beautifully demonstrated the true essence of gift-giving and sharing blessings during the holiday season.

The "Salin Kaalaman: E-Barangay" extension project was a targeted outreach initiative designed to bring digital transformation to grassroots communities in Lopez, Quezon. Running from August to December 2023, the program focused on upskilling 30 Barangay Health Workers and 9 Barangay Officials from the barangays of Hondagua, Matinik, and San Jose. Led by Engr. Maria Luisa N. Francisco and a team from PUP Lopez, participants received hands-on computer literacy training, mastering essential tools like Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and internet navigation.

The project's ultimate highlight was the development and turnover of a custom "E-Barangay" Web-Based Management System for each participating community. This new digital platform was built to modernize local governance by automating record-keeping, processing online document requests, and making crucial community information easily accessible to residents.
