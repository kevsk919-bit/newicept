Extension Programs

This extension program is a community-driven initiative dedicated to empowering residents across various barangays in Lopez, Quezon. Its primary mission is to equip locals with practical knowledge and valuable skills that promote economic self-sufficiency. By focusing on accessible entrepreneurship, the program directly guides participants on how to successfully start and manage their own small businesses using minimal capital. Ultimately, this initiative serves as a practical stepping stone for grassroots economic growth and sustainable livelihood improvement within the community.
